# I&A Formal storefront — guidance for Claude Code

Static React storefront (Vite + Tailwind) on GitHub Pages; Shopify will be the commerce backend, as on iana-schoolwear and iana-fashions. The brand is **I & A Formal**; the repo name and URL stay `iana-tailoring`.

## Rules
1. **Facts come from `src/data/site.ts`; products from `src/data/catalogue.json`.** Confirmed: delivery and returns (`SITE.delivery`, `SITE.returns`), made to measure (`SITE.madeToMeasure`: from £250, 50% deposit, usually 4 to 6 weeks, 2 fittings) and walk-in appointments. Never invent prices, policies, cloth claims, lead times or alteration prices. Unconfirmed items go in `SITE.toConfirm` and appear on pages only as a body-size `[PLACEHOLDER]`, never in a heading or display number. While `SITE.preview` is true, products are samples and must stay labelled as such.
2. **Commerce goes through `src/lib/store.ts` only.** Components never fetch product data directly. "Finish the outfit" logic is `store.related()`.
3. **Every page sets `<Seo>`.** Add new routes to `scripts/prerender.mjs`.
4. **No AI-generated faces.** Models are shown from behind or cropped at the chin. One photo per product, never reused on another product or as a category tile, never twice on one page. A product without a photo gets a `PhotoSlot` (`src/components/Art.tsx`): brand ground, line drawing, caps label, one line on the shot to take.
5. **Brand (I&A guide 2022, Formal variant):** Ochre #45353a is the ground for hero, panels and footer (#2a2326 deep); Sage #d8e2dc for product backdrops, bands and the women's pages; warm white #fbfbf9 for shop pages. No pink on this site. Lockups `public/img/logo/iana-formal-*.svg` come from `/home/claude/brand/scripts/logo.py` with the word FORMAL. Cormorant Garamond Bold headings (sentence case); Agrandir body (400) and 0.15em uppercase labels, Lexend until licensed (change `--font-sans` only). Corners 3px. No pills, gradients as decoration, or card grids for prose. Element rules in `index.css` live in `@layer base` / `@layer components`.
6. **Men, women and kids in equal measure.** Nav: Men, Women, Kids, Made to measure, Alterations, Visit. Women's includes occasion, prom and bridal. Men's suits are sold with the jacket (chest + S/R/L) and trousers (waist + leg) in different sizes, framed as new; the shopper's saved sizes from Find my sizes are marked on every men's suit, but it is not the hero. Appointments are walk-in: no booking forms or slot pickers. Suits, bridal and prom dresses are not returnable and their product pages say so.
7. Tap targets ≥ 44px, no horizontal overflow at 375px, body text ≥ 16px (12–14px only for labels, captions, legal lines), headline visible on the first phone screen, filters behind one button below `lg`, `<html lang="en-GB">`, British English, GBP.
