# I&A Formal storefront — guidance for Claude Code

Static React storefront (Vite + Tailwind) on GitHub Pages; Shopify will be the commerce backend, as on iana-schoolwear and iana-fashions. The brand is **I & A Formal**; the repo name and URL stay `iana-tailoring`.

The page model is a **tailor's catalogue**: centred and symmetrical, titled in engraved capitals with a serif body, pieces shown as plates. It must not look like the other I&A shops (Schoolwear is an order form, Fashions a lookbook, Parfums a gallery wall). Do not borrow their chrome, their colours or their left-aligned layouts.

## Rules
1. **Facts come from `src/data/site.ts`; products from `src/data/catalogue.json`.** Confirmed: delivery and returns (`SITE.delivery`, `SITE.returns`), made to measure (`SITE.madeToMeasure`: from £250, 50% deposit, usually 4 to 6 weeks, 2 fittings) and walk-in appointments. Never invent prices, policies, cloth claims, lead times or alteration prices. Unconfirmed items go in `SITE.toConfirm` and appear on pages only as a body-size `[PLACEHOLDER]`, never in a heading. While `SITE.preview` is true, products are samples and the listing line, the product page and the footer say so.
2. **No stock claims.** We have no stock data. The size matrix shows what can be chosen; nothing says "in stock", "low" or "sold out", and `soldOut` in the catalogue is not read. The one size-aware fact is `cutIn()` in `src/lib/store.ts`: whether a suit or trousers is cut in the shopper's saved jacket length and leg.
3. **Commerce goes through `src/lib/store.ts` only.** Components never fetch product data directly. "To finish the outfit" is `store.related()`.
4. **Every page sets `<Seo>`.** Add new routes to `scripts/prerender.mjs`.
5. **Pictures. No generated photography, ever: no image generation of any kind.** The five v7 photographs (charcoal suit, navy cuff, shirts, women's black suit, swatches) were generated and were removed in v8 with their variants and cards. Until the client's own studio photographs arrive, every piece, the made-to-measure cloth and the shop front are drawn (`Engraving` in `src/components/Art.tsx`), chosen by the product's `slot.art`; `slot.line` says what the photograph will show. A real photograph, when it comes, is one per product and never reused for another, and goes in the product's `images` (it then leads over the drawing). No faces, no street or lifestyle scenes, no stock people. Every picture sits in a `.frame`: one hairline, a 12px mount, then the picture at 3:4.
6. **Colour (Formal owns green):** Bottle green `#1f322b` for the header, the footer, the fitting line and the main button; Sage `#d8e2dc` as its light partner (the ground of every drawing); Chalk `#f7f6f1` reading ground; Ochre `#45353a` text on light. Brass `#a8843f` is a rule and a marker only (the rule under the header and above the footer, the selected size, the saved-size mark, the clip on the tape): never text, never a fill behind text. No pink, no brown grounds.
7. **Type (v5, engraved capitals):** Formal owns one class of letter that no other I&A shop uses: tracked capitals, like a tailor's fascia and stationery. **Marcellus** 400 (the only weight; no faux bold), UPPERCASE, for h1, h2, h3, plate names, door names, the stage names on the fitting line and the header departments (`.navlink`). Set small and wide, never big: h1 22 to 36px tracked 0.09em, the Home title up to 40px, h2 19 to 24px tracked 0.11em, plate names and h3 16 to 19px and the nav 14px tracked 0.14em. Every caps element carries a leading pad equal to its tracking (`--track`) so centred lines stay centred. A caps line stays under about 28 characters (`max-inline-size` on h1, h2 and `.display`, with balanced wrapping): shorten a heading that needs more than three lines. **Libre Caslon Text** 400/700 for all reading text (lowercase, 17px, lede 18 to 19px), links, toggles, table heads and buttons; buttons are Caslon Text bold in sentence case, never caps. **Hanken Grotesk** 400/600 (`.num`) only for sizes, prices, telephone numbers and form fields, including a size inside a caps line. Never capitals for body, links, buttons or anything longer than a title. No italics, no eyebrows above headings, no middle-dot strings, no arrows on links. Libre Caslon Display was dropped: do not bring back a large lowercase serif title (that class belongs to Fashions). Change faces through `--font-display`, `--font-serif`, `--font-sans` only.
8. **Shape:** everything is centred. Corners are 0. Titles sit between double rules (`.rule2`: two hairlines 3px apart), the capitals between them like a shop fascia, via `Title` and `Heading` in `Layout.tsx`. Single hairlines (`--color-hair`) separate plates and rows. No cards, no chips, no pills, no shadows, no gradients as decoration, no sticky phone bar, no hamburger.
9. **Men, women and kids in equal measure.** Nav: Men, Women, Kids, Made to measure, Alterations, Visit. Men's suits are sold as a jacket (chest by length) and trousers (waist by leg), each chosen on its own; the shopper's saved sizes from Find my sizes are marked on every men's suit. Prom and bridal are visit-led: those pages open with "come in and try" and the walk-in facts. Appointments are walk-in: no booking forms or slot pickers. Suits, bridal and prom dresses are not returnable and their pages say so in one plain sentence.
10. **Voice:** a tailor speaking. Full sentences, no quips, no slogans. "Come in and we will measure you." State each fact once per page.
11. Tap targets ≥ 44px, no horizontal overflow at 375px, body text ≥ 16px (15px only for captions and the legal lines), one `h1`, `<html lang="en-GB">`, British English, GBP, AA contrast.

## Design system (v5 type on the v4 catalogue)
- **Header** (`Header`): two centred tiers on every width, both green. The white lockup stands alone on the first (with the hours and the saved sizes at its sides from `lg`); the departments run between a double rule and the brass rule on the second, which sticks to the top of the window and scrolls sideways on a phone. **Footer:** green, centred: the address and telephone, a row of links, the delivery and legal lines.
- **Type scale:** `--t-hero`, `--t-title` (h1), `--t-section` (h2), `--t-plate`, `--t-sub` (h3), `--t-nav`, `--t-lede`, `--t-body`, `--t-small` and the tracking steps `--track-title`, `--track-section`, `--track-small` in `index.css`. Never hard-code a heading size or tracking in a page; `.display` gives any other element the capitals.
- **Home:** the title, then the three doors (Men, Women, Kids: three framed drawings side by side at every width), the fitting line on green, the jacket-and-trouser note, the walk-in paragraph.
- **Listing** (`Shop.tsx`, `Plate.tsx`): plates. Each piece is a full-width row with the framed picture on one side and its specification (`.spec`: cloth, colour, fit, sizes, price) on the other, sides alternating. No sidebar, no grid of cards. Filters are text toggles (`.opt`) in the title block: on Men the saved-size toggle and the suit fit; on Kids the kind of piece.
- **Product page:** one centred column: title, framed picture, price and description, the size matrix (`Matrix`: chest by length, waist by leg; a row of `Keys` for UK sizes, ages, collars and sleeves), the choice in words, one green button, the not-returnable sentence, then delivery, cloth, sizing and alterations as plain paragraphs and the related pieces as ruled text rows. On phones the matrix turns on its side so every key stays 44px.
- **The tape appears once:** the fitting line (`FittingLine` in `Tape.tsx`, on Home and `/made-to-measure`). Five stages over sixty inches; tap, arrow keys or drag. It carries only the facts in `SITE.madeToMeasure`; the week of each fitting is a placeholder. Do not put the tape anywhere else.
- **Drawings** (`Art.tsx`): one hand for every piece: a 300 by 400 plate, a single line weight, the garment on a dressmaker's stand or hanger, ruled shading (`H`) in the folds, knit and lace as fine horizontal rules (`K`). Keys: `jacket` (suit jacket on a stand), `suit` (jacket and trousers on a hanger), `waistcoat` (three piece, the waistcoat ruled), `trousers`, `formal-shirt`, `shirt` (with bow tie), `tie`, `womens-suit`, `dress`, `party`, `gown`, `bridal`, `jumpsuit`, `swatches` (a waterfall bunch of cloth), `shopfront`. A child's piece is the same drawing at nine-tenths scale on the same floor. A new kind of garment needs a new `Art` key drawn in this style.
- **Motion:** tokens `--ease-out`, `--dur-1/2/3`. Motion answers an action (`.swap` on the fitting line, the tape fill and clip). The one unprompted moment is the three doors opening on Home (`.rise`). Product links use `VLink`; a piece's frame on a plate and on its page share `garmentVT(handle)`. Reduced motion switches all of it off.

## Performance and mobile (v6)
- **Fonts are served from this site.** `@fontsource/marcellus`, `libre-caslon-text` and `hanken-grotesk`, latin woff2 only, declared by hand at the top of `index.css` with `font-display: swap`. No Google Fonts link. Marcellus 400 and Caslon Text 400 are preloaded by the `preload-fonts` plugin in `vite.config.ts`. Each face has a metric-matched fallback (`Marcellus Fallback`, `Caslon Fallback`, `Hanken Fallback`, plus Noto Serif and Roboto versions for Android) so nothing re-breaks when the face arrives; if a face or weight changes, recompute `size-adjust` and the overrides (capitals for Marcellus, figures for Hanken).
- **Photographs come in widths.** There are none on the site today (v8); the pipeline stays for the client's own. `scripts/images.mjs` (run by `npm run build` and `npm run images`) skips a missing folder, cuts every `public/img/{looks,products}/<name>.webp` to 320, 480, 640 and 800px and writes `src/data/images.json` (empty now); commit the variants. Pages use `photo(path)` and `SIZES` from `store.ts` for `src`, `srcSet`, `sizes`, `width` and `height`; never point an `<img>` at the source file. Every `<img>` has `decoding="async"` and is `loading="lazy"`, except the first picture of a listing or product page (`first`: eager, `fetchPriority="high"`).
- **Pages are hydrated, not redrawn.** `scripts/prerender.mjs` writes each route with `renderToString` (through `window.__prerender` in `main.tsx`), and `main.tsx` hydrates when the markup is for the current path. So the first render must not read the device: saved sizes are read in a layout effect (`sizes.tsx`), `index.html` sets `html.has-sizes` before paint, and a plate keeps its `.mine` line for them. Choices on a page (listing filters, chosen sizes) go through `useKept` (`src/lib/kept.ts`) so Back returns the page as it was left; `Scroll` in `App.tsx` restores the scroll position on Back, Forward and reload.
- **Safe areas.** The page margin is `--gutter`, and `.wrap` and `.navlist` never go inside `env(safe-area-inset-left/right)`; the header pads the top inset and the footer the bottom one. There are no fixed bars; do not add one.
- **Landscape phones.** The sticky departments are the only sticky thing; in `(orientation: landscape) and (max-height: 480px)` they drop to 44px. `--nav-h` is what anchors and focused fields clear (`scroll-margin`).
- **The departments row on phones** scrolls sideways with snap and `overscroll-behavior-x: contain`, brings the current department into view, and its gap steps by width so the right edge always cuts a word (the cue that the row goes on). If a department is renamed, re-check the cut at 320, 360, 375, 390, 412 and 430.
- **Touch.** Hover styles live in the `@media (hover: hover)` block at the foot of `index.css`; every control has an `:active` state with no fade. Controls carry `touch-action: manipulation`. A matrix key's `aria-label` starts with its visible text.
- **Motion.** Only `transform` and `opacity` move (the tape fill and clip included). View transitions finish inside 320ms and are skipped when the page is hidden or motion is reduced. On a product page the choice that completes the size brings the button into view; nothing else scrolls the page for the shopper.
- Measure with `/home/claude/builds/v6/tools/sweep.cjs` and the scripts in `/home/claude/builds/v6/iana-tailoring/` (`lh.sh`, `perf.cjs`, `walk.cjs`, `shots.cjs`).

## SEO and sharing (v7)
Every link must work and share fully on GitHub Pages (`https://sheedosa.github.io/iana-tailoring/`). Checked with `/home/claude/builds/v7/seo/_scripts/` (`audit.mjs`, `pages-test.mjs`, `share.mjs`, `lh.sh`); results in `/home/claude/builds/v7/seo/iana-tailoring/`.
- **Not indexed yet.** `SITE.indexable` in `src/data/site.ts` is `false` while the site is a preview: `Seo` gives every page `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, follow">` (the 404 page keeps `noindex,nofollow`), and everything else below stays as it is. `robots.txt` allows all, so link-preview bots still read the pages; never add `Disallow` there. Flip `indexable` to `true` when the client confirms the facts and wants the site in search.
- **Addresses end in a slash.** Pages serves `/route/` and 301s `/route` to it, so canonicals, `og:url`, breadcrumbs, the sitemap and `llms.txt` use `pageUrl(path)` from `Seo.tsx`. The sitemap lists every route in `scripts/prerender.mjs` and nothing else.
- **Every page's `<Seo>` gives, in the prerendered HTML:** a unique `<title>` under 60 characters, page first and "I&A Formal" last; a unique description of 120 to 160 characters built with `fit()` from `SITE` and the catalogue (no brackets, no prices: they are placeholders); one canonical; the full Open Graph set (`og:type`, `og:site_name`, `og:title`, `og:description`, `og:url`, absolute `og:image`, 1200x630 width and height, `og:image:alt`, `og:locale` en_GB) and Twitter `summary_large_image`. The 404 page is `noindex` with no canonical.
- **The card.** `public/img/og/og-home.jpg` (lockup, the title between double rules, the three framed drawings on green). Every piece has its own card in the same arrangement with its framed drawing at 3:4, read from its built page (`public/img/og/p/<handle>.jpg`, made by `_scripts/cards.mjs` with `cards-iana-tailoring.mjs` from a served `dist`; `store.card()` picks it). Re-run it after changing a drawing or adding a product, then build again. No card is ever made from a generated image; never point `og:image` at a product photograph directly. Keep cards under 300 KB.
- **Structured data allowed:** `ClothingStore` (address, telephone, hours, returns window from `SITE`) on every page, `WebSite` on Home, `BreadcrumbList` on inner pages, `CollectionPage` on listings, `Service` on made to measure (the one confirmed price, from £250) and alterations, `Product` with name, description, image, material, colour and brand. **No `offers` on products**: there are no confirmed prices. No placeholder or unconfirmed fact in JSON-LD.
- **Head:** `lang="en-GB"`, theme colour Bottle green `#1f322b`, SVG favicon plus `favicon-32.png`, 180px `apple-touch-icon`, `site.webmanifest` with `scope`, 192, 512 and a maskable 512 icon (`_scripts/icons.mjs` from the sage marque). Every path carries `/iana-tailoring/`.
- **Deep links:** `public/404.html` (noindex) sends an unknown path to `/?p=`, `main.tsx` restores it, and a path no route matches shows `NotFound`. `public/.nojekyll` is deployed. Search engines read only `https://sheedosa.github.io/robots.txt` for a project site; ours names the sitemap for tools that look.

## UX evidence (v9)
Every design or wording change names its evidence: a published standard or study (GOV.UK Service Manual and Design System, Baymard, WCAG 2.2) or a measurement taken on this site. No change on taste alone. Measured in November 2026 on every route at 390 and 1280 with `/home/claude/builds/v9/measure.cjs` (results in `/home/claude/builds/v9/`) and `forms.cjs`.
- **Reading level.** Prose is written for a reading age of about 9 (GOV.UK content design): sentences of 25 words or fewer, one idea each.
- **Kept on purpose:** one h1 per page, no vague button or link label, no unnamed control, no sideways overflow, every field with a visible label, and an empty form marks the fields to fix, describes each one and moves focus to the first.
- **Not done yet:** nobody has been watched using the site. Before launch, watch three to five real customers do the main task on their own phones.
- **This pass:** two sentences over 25 words (Home, Find my sizes) were split; the facts are unchanged.
- **The size matrix** stays one choice at a time (chest, then length; waist, then leg): GOV.UK's one thing per page.
