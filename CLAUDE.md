# I&A Formal storefront — guidance for Claude Code

Static React storefront (Vite + Tailwind) on GitHub Pages; Shopify will be the commerce backend, as on iana-schoolwear and iana-fashions. The brand is **I & A Formal**; the repo name and URL stay `iana-tailoring`.

The page model is a **tailor's catalogue**: centred and symmetrical, serif-bodied, pieces shown as plates. It must not look like the other I&A shops (Schoolwear is an order form, Fashions a lookbook, Parfums a gallery wall). Do not borrow their chrome, their colours or their left-aligned layouts.

## Rules
1. **Facts come from `src/data/site.ts`; products from `src/data/catalogue.json`.** Confirmed: delivery and returns (`SITE.delivery`, `SITE.returns`), made to measure (`SITE.madeToMeasure`: from £250, 50% deposit, usually 4 to 6 weeks, 2 fittings) and walk-in appointments. Never invent prices, policies, cloth claims, lead times or alteration prices. Unconfirmed items go in `SITE.toConfirm` and appear on pages only as a body-size `[PLACEHOLDER]`, never in a heading. While `SITE.preview` is true, products are samples and the listing line, the product page and the footer say so.
2. **No stock claims.** We have no stock data. The size matrix shows what can be chosen; nothing says "in stock", "low" or "sold out", and `soldOut` in the catalogue is not read. The one size-aware fact is `cutIn()` in `src/lib/store.ts`: whether a suit or trousers is cut in the shopper's saved jacket length and leg.
3. **Commerce goes through `src/lib/store.ts` only.** Components never fetch product data directly. "To finish the outfit" is `store.related()`.
4. **Every page sets `<Seo>`.** Add new routes to `scripts/prerender.mjs`.
5. **Pictures.** No AI-generated faces, no street or lifestyle scenes, no stock people. A piece is shown by its own studio photograph (one per product, never reused for another product) or, until that exists, by its drawing (`Engraving` in `src/components/Art.tsx`). Every picture sits in a `.frame`: one hairline, a 12px mount, then the picture at 3:4.
6. **Colour (Formal owns green):** Bottle green `#1f322b` for the header, the footer, the fitting line and the main button; Sage `#d8e2dc` as its light partner (the ground of every drawing); Chalk `#f7f6f1` reading ground; Ochre `#45353a` text on light. Brass `#a8843f` is a rule and a marker only (the rule under the header and above the footer, the selected size, the saved-size mark, the clip on the tape): never text, never a fill behind text. No pink, no brown grounds.
7. **Type:** Libre Caslon Display for h1, h2 and names (one weight, sentence case); Libre Caslon Text 400/700 for all reading text, links and buttons; Hanken Grotesk (`.num`) only for sizes, prices, telephone numbers and form fields. No italics, no caps labels, no eyebrows above headings, no middle-dot strings, no arrows on links. Change faces through `--font-display`, `--font-serif`, `--font-sans` only.
8. **Shape:** everything is centred. Corners are 0. Titles sit between double rules (`.rule2`: two hairlines 3px apart) via `Title` and `Heading` in `Layout.tsx`. Single hairlines (`--color-hair`) separate plates and rows. No cards, no chips, no pills, no shadows, no gradients as decoration, no sticky phone bar, no hamburger.
9. **Men, women and kids in equal measure.** Nav: Men, Women, Kids, Made to measure, Alterations, Visit. Men's suits are sold as a jacket (chest by length) and trousers (waist by leg), each chosen on its own; the shopper's saved sizes from Find my sizes are marked on every men's suit. Prom and bridal are visit-led: those pages open with "come in and try" and the walk-in facts. Appointments are walk-in: no booking forms or slot pickers. Suits, bridal and prom dresses are not returnable and their pages say so in one plain sentence.
10. **Voice:** a tailor speaking. Full sentences, no quips, no slogans. "Come in and we will measure you." State each fact once per page.
11. Tap targets ≥ 44px, no horizontal overflow at 375px, body text ≥ 16px (15px only for captions and the legal lines), one `h1`, `<html lang="en-GB">`, British English, GBP, AA contrast.

## Design system (v4)
- **Header** (`Header`): two centred tiers on every width, both green. The white lockup stands alone on the first (with the hours and the saved sizes at its sides from `lg`); the departments run between a double rule and the brass rule on the second, which sticks to the top of the window and scrolls sideways on a phone. **Footer:** green, centred: the address and telephone, a row of links, the delivery and legal lines.
- **Type scale:** `--t-hero`, `--t-title` (h1), `--t-section` (h2), `--t-plate`, `--t-sub` (h3), `--t-lede`, `--t-body`, `--t-small` in `index.css`. Never hard-code a heading size in a page.
- **Home:** the title, then the three doors (Men, Women, Kids: three framed drawings side by side at every width), the fitting line on green, the jacket-and-trouser note, the walk-in paragraph.
- **Listing** (`Shop.tsx`, `Plate.tsx`): plates. Each piece is a full-width row with the framed picture on one side and its specification (`.spec`: cloth, colour, fit, sizes, price) on the other, sides alternating. No sidebar, no grid of cards. Filters are text toggles (`.opt`) in the title block: on Men the saved-size toggle and the suit fit; on Kids the kind of piece.
- **Product page:** one centred column: title, framed picture, price and description, the size matrix (`Matrix`: chest by length, waist by leg; a row of `Keys` for UK sizes, ages, collars and sleeves), the choice in words, one green button, the not-returnable sentence, then delivery, cloth, sizing and alterations as plain paragraphs and the related pieces as ruled text rows. On phones the matrix turns on its side so every key stays 44px.
- **The tape appears once:** the fitting line (`FittingLine` in `Tape.tsx`, on Home and `/made-to-measure`). Five stages over sixty inches; tap, arrow keys or drag. It carries only the facts in `SITE.madeToMeasure`; the week of each fitting is a placeholder. Do not put the tape anywhere else.
- **Drawings** (`Art.tsx`): one hand for every piece: a 300 by 400 plate, a single line weight, the garment on a dressmaker's stand or hanger, ruled shading (`H`) in the folds, knit and lace as fine horizontal rules (`K`). A child's piece is the same drawing at four-fifths scale. A new kind of garment needs a new `Art` key drawn in this style.
- **Motion:** tokens `--ease-out`, `--dur-1/2/3`. Motion answers an action (`.swap` on the fitting line, the tape fill and clip). The one unprompted moment is the three doors opening on Home (`.rise`). Product links use `VLink`; a piece's frame on a plate and on its page share `garmentVT(handle)`. Reduced motion switches all of it off.
