# I&A Formal storefront — guidance for Claude Code

Static React storefront (Vite + Tailwind) on GitHub Pages; Shopify will be the commerce backend, as on iana-schoolwear and iana-fashions. The brand is **I & A Formal**; the repo name and URL stay `iana-tailoring`.

## Rules
1. **Facts come from `src/data/site.ts`; products from `src/data/catalogue.json`.** Confirmed: delivery and returns (`SITE.delivery`, `SITE.returns`), made to measure (`SITE.madeToMeasure`: from £250, 50% deposit, usually 4 to 6 weeks, 2 fittings) and walk-in appointments. Never invent prices, policies, cloth claims, lead times or alteration prices. Unconfirmed items go in `SITE.toConfirm` and appear on pages only as a body-size `[PLACEHOLDER]`, never in a heading or display number. While `SITE.preview` is true, products are samples and must stay labelled as such.
2. **Commerce goes through `src/lib/store.ts` only.** Components never fetch product data directly. "Finish the outfit" logic is `store.related()`.
3. **Every page sets `<Seo>`.** Add new routes to `scripts/prerender.mjs`.
4. **No AI-generated faces.** Models are shown from behind or cropped at the chin. One photo per product, never reused on another product and never twice on one page. The one exception is the home triptych: each of the three panels (Men, Women, Kids) shows one piece from that side of the shop, and the same photo must not appear elsewhere on Home. A product without a photo gets a `PhotoSlot` (`src/components/Art.tsx`): brand ground, line drawing, caps label, one line on the shot to take.
5. **Brand (I&A guide 2022, Formal variant):** Ochre #45353a is the ground for hero, the fitting line, panels and the visit band (#2a2326 deep for the strip and footer); Sage #d8e2dc for product backdrops, bands and the women's pages; warm white #fbfbf9 for shop pages. No pink on this site. Lockups `public/img/logo/iana-formal-*.svg` come from `/home/claude/brand/scripts/logo.py` with the word FORMAL and do not change. **Faces:** Libre Caslon Display (one weight) for h1 and h2, sentence case, size carries the hierarchy; Libre Caslon Text (400, 400 italic, 700) for h3 / `.t-sub` and the italic `.voice` line; Hanken Grotesk 400/500/600 for text and controls (change `--font-display`, `--font-serif`, `--font-sans` only). Corners 3px. No pills, gradients as decoration, drop shadows, or card grids for prose. Element rules in `index.css` live in `@layer base` / `@layer components`.
6. **Men, women and kids in equal measure.** Nav: Men, Women, Kids, Made to measure, Alterations, Visit. Women's includes occasion, prom and bridal. Men's suits are sold with the jacket (chest + S/R/L) and trousers (waist + leg) in different sizes, framed as new; the shopper's saved sizes from Find my sizes are marked on every men's suit, but it is not the hero. Appointments are walk-in: no booking forms or slot pickers. Suits, bridal and prom dresses are not returnable and their product pages say so.
7. Tap targets ≥ 44px, no horizontal overflow at 375px, body text ≥ 16px (12–14px only for labels, captions, legal lines), headline visible on the first phone screen, filters behind one button below `lg`, `<html lang="en-GB">`, British English, GBP.

## Design system (v3)
- **Type:** one fluid scale in `index.css` (`--t-hero`, `--t-title`, `--t-section`, `--t-sub`, `--t-lede`, `--t-body`, `--t-small`). `h1`, `h2`, `h3` take their size from it; `.t-hero` is the only override pages use. Never hard-code a heading size. `.voice` (Caslon Text italic) is the short second line under a title or a name; do not set figures with a slash in it. Caps `.label` is for controls, table heads and footer headings only: no eyebrows above headings, no middle-dot strings, no arrows on links. Buttons are sentence case.
- **The tape is the one motif** (`src/components/Tape.tsx`, `.tape` in `index.css`): cells are inches, with ticks and numerals. It appears as the fitting line, the size finder's two rulers (`Ruler`), a short length under page titles (`TitleTape`), the divider above the footer and in the phone menu. Nowhere else, and never as a background pattern.
- **Signature: the fitting line** (`FittingLine`, on Home and `/made-to-measure`). Five stages on a 60-inch tape that starts at the page margin and runs off the right edge; tap a stage, use the arrow keys or drag along the tape, and the Sage "measured" length and the clip move to it while the panel below shows its figure and one line. Below `md` the tape runs down the left edge and the chosen stage opens. It carries only the confirmed facts in `SITE.madeToMeasure`; the week of each fitting is a placeholder listed in `toConfirm`. Do not add stage copy the client has not confirmed.
- **Motion:** tokens `--ease-out`, `--dur-1/2/3`. Motion answers an action (`.swap`, the tape fill and clip, the filter sheet). The one unprompted moment is the three home panels arriving (`.rise`). Product links use `VLink`, and the picture on a card and on its page share `garmentVT(handle)`. Reduced motion switches all of it off.
- **Layout:** Home is the headline, the triptych (three tall panels; a swipe rail on phones) standing half on the Ochre ground, the fitting line, the separates band with the size finder, a rail of pieces, the occasions index, then the visit band running into the footer tape. Men, Women and Kids each have their own title (`MenTitle`, `WomenTitle`, `KidsTitle` in `Shop.tsx`); do not merge them into one template.
- **Product page:** sizes are chosen on the fitting card (ruled rows, the choice written on the card's top line, saved sizes marked with a dot). The non-returnable notice is a plain sentence under the button. On phones `StickyBar` shows the name, price line and the action once the button has scrolled away, and steps aside for the footer; the listing uses the same bar for the count and the filter sheet.
